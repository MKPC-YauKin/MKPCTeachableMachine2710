THE FIELD GUIDE

Hands-free, not brain-free.

01

Train two gestures

Open Teachable Machine and choose a Pose Project. Record at least two classes — for example Left hand and Right hand. Add a Neutral class if you can; it gives the model a rest pose and helps prevent accidental answers.

02

Export and bring it here

Choose Export Model → Upload my model to get a share URL, or download the model files. Load either one from the model panel. Everything runs locally in this tab.

03

Match gestures to A and B

In Gesture settings, assign your model's classes to the two answer sides. If your labels are simply “Class 1” and “Class 2”, choose the order that feels natural.

04

Hold, answer, release

Hold your chosen gesture for the set duration. The ring fills up, the answer locks in, and you can lower your hand. Release before trying the next question.

Give yourself some room. Place the camera at chest height and keep your hands in frame.

Light helps. Face a window if you can. Busy backgrounds and backlighting make pose tracking work harder.

Tune the sensitivity. Lower the confidence threshold if gestures are missed; raise the hold time if answers fire too easily.
